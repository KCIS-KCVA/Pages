KCVA — KC Varsity Association — is a student-led interschool varsity sports organization. It connects boys’ and girls’ school teams through structured competition and gives students real responsibility for event operations, communication, partnerships, media and match delivery.

Adult school representatives and venue partners remain essential to school approval, participant safety and event readiness. Verified school and team information is published only after confirmation.
