KCVA is KC Varsity Association, a student-led interschool varsity sports organization. It connects boys’ and girls’ school teams through organized competition and gives students practical responsibility for event operations, communication, media, and match delivery.

What we build

KCVA aims to create competitive, well-organized, and memorable volleyball experiences for school communities. The organization is independent and student-led; it is not described as a registered nonprofit, charity, federation, governing body, or company.

How students lead

Students contribute to tournament planning, school liaison, sponsorship coordination, technical production, content, and on-court operations. Adult school representatives and venue partners remain essential for participant safety, school approval, and event readiness.

Across schools

The format is designed to welcome boys’ teams, girls’ teams, or both divisions from each participating school. Verified school and team information will be published only when confirmed.
