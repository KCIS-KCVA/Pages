About KCVA

More than

Safety & responsibility

Ready for
the rally.

  • Each school appoints one adult teacher or coach as the official team contact.
  • Schools or designated adults retain personal medical and guardian information.
  • KCVA collects only the minimum information needed to organize the event.
  • KCVA does not provide individual accident or medical insurance.
  • Schools should confirm appropriate participant accident and medical coverage.
  • Final first-aid, evacuation, and emergency communication procedures will be published before the event.

FAQ

Court
questions.

Who can participate?

Currently enrolled students representing their own school, subject to the final eligibility rules provided before the tournament.

Can a school enter both boys’ and girls’ teams?

Yes. A school may enter a boys’ team, a girls’ team, or both divisions.

Is the tournament free?

No. It is a cost-sharing event with an estimated fee of approximately RMB 400 per player.

When will the final fee be confirmed?

After registration closes and total participation and actual event costs are confirmed.

What information does KCVA collect?

Only minimum roster and official school-contact information required for eligibility, scheduling, and event operations.

Does KCVA provide participant insurance?

No. Schools are strongly encouraged to confirm suitable accident and medical coverage for their players.

Who should schools contact?

Schools should use the official KCVA contact channel listed on the Contact page and appoint one adult teacher or coach as their team contact.

When will the final schedule be released?

After registration closes and the final number of boys’ and girls’ teams is confirmed.
