GOOD TO KNOW

FAQ.

Who can participate?

Currently enrolled students representing their own school, subject to the final eligibility rules provided before the tournament.

Can a school enter both divisions?

Yes. Schools may enter a boys’ team, a girls’ team or both.

What is the fee?

This is a cost-sharing event. The estimated fee is approximately RMB 400 per player; the final amount depends on confirmed participation and actual event costs.

When will the final schedule and rules be released?

After registration closes and the final number of participating teams is confirmed.

Is adult supervision required?

Each school must appoint an adult teacher or coach as its official team contact.
